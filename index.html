Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery timings for restaurants vs home delivery?

For commercial accounts (restaurants, hotels, cafes), we deliver early mornings between 5:30 AM and 8:00 AM. For home deliveries, orders are dispatched daily between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

Is there a minimum order requirement?

There is no minimum order requirement for household deliveries! For commercial wholesale accounts, custom volume requirements apply—contact us on WhatsApp for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI, Bank Transfers, and flexible weekly or monthly invoicing terms for verified commercial accounts.

How do you ensure produce freshness and cleanliness?

Our produce is sourced daily at 4 AM directly from local farms. Items undergo thorough ozone cleaning and quality grading before being packed in hygienic, food-grade packaging.
